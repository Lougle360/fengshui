# Language routes

## Classical Chinese (`lzh`)

Translate syntax and omitted subjects conservatively. Preserve technical terms when a modern paraphrase would narrow or distort them. Put necessary clarification in brackets or a linked editor note, not as an unmarked source claim.

## Modern Japanese (`ja`)

Translate Japanese grammar, particles, tense/aspect, honorifics, and named entities. Do not read kanji strings as Chinese phrases.

## Classical Japanese (`ja-bungo`)

Account for historical grammar, orthography, auxiliaries, and classical vocabulary. Lower confidence when the route lacks adequate linguistic evidence.

## Kanbun (`kanbun`)

Distinguish original Literary Chinese, reading marks, okurigana, and Japanese kundoku interpretation. State whether the modern Chinese follows the Chinese base text, the Japanese reading tradition, or both.

## Mixed text

Segment by evidenced language boundaries and retain segment IDs under the original block. Footnotes, quotations, and commentary may require different routes within one page.

## Translation record

Record source and normalized text, modern Chinese, route, glossary decisions, alternatives, confidence, and review status. `accepted` means semantically checked, not merely fluent.
